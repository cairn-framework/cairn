# Proposal: agent-guidance-provenance

## Motivation

The agent-guidance programme cannot move loop authority until a refining
decision is proposed with honest external provenance. The Harness Engineering
anthology supplies mechanism evidence, but that evidence must be pinned,
scoped, and bounded before any later router migration is sanctioned.

## Scope

- Capture the pinned Harness Engineering source at commit
  `226c8d35fb6ea3ed55467753dba6dea2b5fd5778` with the six concrete reviewed
  paths.
- Distil the five load-bearing mechanisms into linked research with precise
  validator and proof limits.
- Propose `dec.unified-cairn-dev-entry` as a refining decision that preserves
  every clause of `dec.loop-command-harness-model`, keeps
  `status: proposed`, and sanctions only a later migration.
- Keep external scheduling, repetition, runtime, and parallelism outside Cairn
  under `dec.no-orchestrator`.

## Out of scope

- Accepting the refining decision.
- Superseding or marking `dec.loop-command-harness-model` superseded.
- Moving loop authority on acceptance alone.
- Implementing router, pack, query, adapter, or runtime changes.
- Opening a second normative loop authority.

## Acceptance

- `meta/sources/harness-engineering.md` pins the commit and lists the six
  concrete paths.
- `meta/research/harness-engineering.md` covers all five mechanisms with
  precise limits and cites the source.
- `meta/decisions/unified-cairn-dev-entry.md` remains `status: proposed`,
  formally refines `dec.loop-command-harness-model`, restates all eight
  clauses, and states that acceptance sanctions later migration without moving
  authority now.
- No production code, pack, adapter, or query surface changes.
- `cairn scan` introduces no new findings from these artefacts.
